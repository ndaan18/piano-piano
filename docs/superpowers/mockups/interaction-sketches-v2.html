8 · Reference: the grammar book

Phone: same pages in a single column. The bottom tab bar is Path · Review · Reference, and the Check button sits right above the keyboard. Leaving mid-lesson saves your exact step, so "Resume" picks up there.

Try it: the practice card (page 5)

This one works. Enter checks, Enter continues. Try a typo, a missing accent, or a wrong form on purpose. Sound on for the listening card.

✕

One thing at a time. A single focused card, with the keyboard as the main control: Enter checks, Enter continues, Space plays audio. On the phone the Check button sticks above the keyboard.

Forgiving where it should be. A one-letter typo counts as correct but is pointed out. A missing accent counts as "almost": it's accepted, but you're told why the accent matters. Real grammar mistakes get explained.

Accents are easy. Tap the accent bar, or type the letter followed by a backtick, e.g. e` → è.

Mistakes come back. A wrong item reappears 2–3 cards later in the same session (the orange segment), then goes into your review queue (repeating after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days).

You can overrule the checker. "I was right" accepts your answer and saves it as a valid alternative for that exercise from then on.

Hover (or tap) any dotted Italian word to see what it means. Translation is always one gesture away, never shown by default.
