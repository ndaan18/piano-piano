A blur system, not a blur

Every unit cover is generated from its own recipe: a different composition, colour pairing, softness and grain, seeded by the unit number so it's always the same for that unit. Here are all 19, at the size they'll appear. They also drift very slowly (off when your system has reduced motion turned on).

8 compositions. Orb, horizon, diagonal, column, eclipse, scatter, rise, halo. No two neighbouring units share one.

Lime is always there, but its share varies from a thin rim to the whole field. The partner colour rotates so neighbours never match.

Softness varies from hazy (≈70px blur) to almost sharp-edged (≈18px). That's what stops it all reading as the same "AI gradient".

Checkpoint units go dark: neon on ink. Every few units the rhythm breaks, which marks a milestone.
